# Privacy Policy for Phishing Guard Extension

**Last Updated:** February 27, 2026

## Overview

Phishing Guard is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our browser extension handles data.

## Data Collection

**Phishing Guard does NOT collect any personal data.**

### What We Don't Collect:
- ❌ Personal identification information
- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ URLs you visit
- ❌ Login credentials
- ❌ Cookies or session data
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Contact information
- ❌ Any data sent to external servers

### What We Store Locally:
- ✅ Extension settings (enabled/disabled state)
- ✅ Count of threats detected (stored only on your device)
- ✅ List of recently detected threats (stored only on your device, max 100 entries)

All stored data remains on your local device and is never transmitted anywhere.

## How the Extension Works

Phishing Guard operates entirely within your browser:

1. **Local Analysis**: All URL scanning happens locally in your browser
2. **No External APIs**: No data is sent to external servers for analysis
3. **Offline Operation**: Works completely offline without internet
4. **No Telemetry**: No usage statistics or crash reports are collected

## Permissions Explained

The extension requests these permissions solely for functionality:

- **activeTab**: To scan links on the current webpage
- **storage**: To save your settings locally
- **notifications**: To alert you about detected threats
- **scripting**: To highlight links on web pages
- **host_permissions**: To scan links on all websites you visit

## Third-Party Services

Phishing Guard does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking.

## Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device:
- Your data cannot be accessed by us
- Your data cannot be sold or shared
- Your data cannot be breached from our servers
- You have complete control over your data

## Children's Privacy

Phishing Guard does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
