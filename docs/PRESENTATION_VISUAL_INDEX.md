## 🎨 COLOR QUICK REFERENCE

### Primary Palette:
```
Deep Blue:     #1e3a8a (Headers, trust)
Blue:          #3b82f6 (Links, secondary)
Light Blue:    #dbeafe (Backgrounds)

Green:         #10b981 (Safe, success, metrics)
Light Green:   #d1fae5 (Success backgrounds)

Orange:        #f59e0b (AI-generated, warnings)
Light Orange:  #ffedd5 (Warning backgrounds)

Red:           #ef4444 (Phishing, danger, errors)
Light Red:     #fee2e2 (Danger backgrounds)

Gray:          #6b7280 (Secondary text)
Light Gray:    #f3f4f6 (Backgrounds)

Gold:          #fbbf24 (Stars, highlights)
```

### Usage Guidelines:
- **Titles:** Deep Blue or White on dark backgrounds
- **Success metrics:** Green with white text
- **Warnings:** Orange with dark text
- **Errors/Danger:** Red with white text
- **Backgrounds:** Light colors (blue, green, gray)
- **Highlights:** Gold for key achievements

## 📐 SLIDE DIMENSIONS & FORMATTING

### Standard Slide Size:
- **PowerPoint:** 16:9 widescreen (33.867cm x 19.05cm)
- **Google Slides:** 16:9 (1920 x 1080 pixels)

### Font Sizes:
- **Title:** 36-48pt (Bold)
- **Subtitle:** 24-32pt
- **Body text:** 18-24pt
- **Captions:** 14-18pt
- **Code:** 12-16pt (Monospace)

### Spacing:
- **Margin:** 1-2cm from edges
- **Line spacing:** 1.5x for readability
- **Bullet spacing:** 0.5-1cm between items
