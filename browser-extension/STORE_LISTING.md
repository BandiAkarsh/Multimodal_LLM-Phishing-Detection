## Detailed Description

🛡️ **Protect Yourself from Phishing Attacks - Instantly!**

Phishing Guard is your intelligent browser companion that automatically scans every link you encounter and warns you about potential phishing threats. Powered by advanced AI-based detection algorithms, it keeps you safe without compromising your privacy.

### ✨ Key Features

**🔍 Automatic Protection**
- Scans ALL links on every webpage in real-time
- Instantly highlights dangerous links with color-coded warnings
- Detects typosquatting attacks (fake domains like paypa1.com)
- Identifies suspicious URL patterns automatically

**🎨 Visual Warning System**
- 🟢 **Green**: Safe/Legitimate links
- 🟠 **Orange**: Suspicious links - proceed with caution
- 🔴 **Red**: Phishing detected - DO NOT CLICK
- 🔵 **Blue**: Currently scanning

**⚡ Quick Scan Tool**
- Manually scan any URL before clicking
- Get instant risk assessment with confidence scores
- View detailed threat indicators
- Perfect for checking suspicious emails and messages

**🔒 Privacy First**
- ✅ **100% Offline** - works without internet
- ✅ **Zero Data Collection** - nothing leaves your device
- ✅ **No External APIs** - runs entirely in your browser
- ✅ **No Registration Required** - install and protect immediately

### 🧠 Smart Detection

Our multi-layered detection system identifies:
- **Typosquatting**: Fake domains impersonating trusted brands
- **Suspicious TLDs**: High-risk domain extensions (.tk, .ml, .xyz)
- **URL Structure**: IP addresses, suspicious keywords, length anomalies
- **HTTPS Validation**: Sites without secure connections
- **Brand Impersonation**: Fake PayPal, Amazon, Google, and more
